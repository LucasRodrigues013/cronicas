## Production workflow
1. Read live HEAD/RESUME, previous real choice, planning (including Calderva after conference/desdobramentos), R33/R34 docs, this contract and approved references. Do not overwrite existing parts; do not choose for the author.
2. Persist R33-compatible request plus r34_intent plus r35_plan before writing. r35_plan schema worldroot-r35-plan-v1: part, profile (regular, special if real choice 4, finale if V), scene_cards aligned with scene_plan, each scene, word_budget positive integer, experience/reaction/payoff contextual descriptions, focus list among R35 FOCUS; must include every R34 planned scene/dimension. Total plan floor 8800/9500/11000. references array >=2 distinct narrative requirement IDs with verified sources. cadence_intent, adult_opportunity, epic_payoff contextual prose. No per-scene word quota; revise budget organically.
3. Write full novel prose in planned segments, not summaries or simultaneous compressed recap. Use scene and reaction craft; action, sensory context, distinct voices, silence, humor and consequences. Important events get preparation, lived action, aftermath. Do not force adversity in rest or romance; do not nerf power. Adult characters: concrete physical appearance, clothing, attraction and frank non-graphic conversations when relevant, not mere hair mention; preserve confirmed ages, evidence, agency and personal identity. Never sexualize minors/unknown ages. Avoid compulsory sexual content. Do not invent old traits. Calderva: recover approved full planning; no surprise destroyed by mechanical timeline. Keep paragraph breathing and dialogue pacing; vary sentence length rather than formatting tricks.
4. Review whole draft, compare historical strong passages and current draft by cadence/emotion/adventure/character/design, correct missed opportunities and condensed key events. Freeze 2–6 final segment files at production/EPISODE/segments/NN.md, concatenate exactly, hash and read back. No extra choices between segments; no false save or completion. If client cannot display complete episode, provide exact successive portions only with real interaction; distinguish staged text from release, never truncate or promise background work. Archive one complete immutable episode, show same full text, confirm receipt.
5. metadata.worldroot_revision=35; render_opening(metadata,opening) and render_choices(packet,id,revision=35); retain r32_review/r33_review/r34_review. r35_review schema worldroot-r35-review-v1: scenes list per actual scene (scene, planned_scene, beats dict experience/reaction/payoff with distinct literal spans >=4 words, assessment); if scene plan changes, scene_plan_change contextual explanation. opportunities dict for every FOCUS with same status as R34; applied moments list >=2 distinct nonoverlapping literal spans (scene,evidence,effect), held context and assessment. comparison for cadence/emotion/adventure/character/design each reference_id from plan, assessment, result retained/improved/contextual_difference; unresolved regressions cannot be released. no_padding and no_condensed_key_events true only after review. R35 structural gate does not certify semantic support or artistic merit.
6. Hard floor 8800 regular, 9500 special, 11000 finale (target ranges 9000–11000, 10000–13000, 12000–15000). Exceptional short part only if the author explicitly writes a line in the genuine choice free_text "AUTORIZO PARTE CURTA: <reason of at least five words>". Never fabricate that approval. Duplicate paragraph/short-block padding >=8% blocks. No upper word limit except existing safety/transport byte limits. Existing episodes untouched; no replay old policy against old receipts. All review receipts bind literal request/prose/segments, no semantic certification.

## Research translated into practice
- Randy Ingermanson, Advanced Fiction Writing, https://www.advancedfictionwriting.com/blog/2017/05/14/start-end-scene/ — action and reaction structure, used flexibly rather than compulsory conflict.
- Writing Excuses, https://writingexcuses.com/writing-excuses-10-44-qa-on-pacing/ — pace and scene/sequel; make the scene worth living, alternate faster and slower passages.
- Brandon Sanderson, https://www.brandonsanderson.com/blogs/blog/writing-excuses-qa-on-pacing-updates — progress and payoff. Do not rush rewards or impose artificial crisis.
- OpenAI, https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/evaluation-best-practices — task-specific evaluation with actual historical inputs, pairwise comparison, human feedback, continuous regression. Automated checks only prove structural behavior.
